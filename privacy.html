1. Introduction

Amazing X Downloader ("the App") is an Android application that helps users organize X (formerly Twitter) post links and download available images and videos. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use the App.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features used, the App may process:

  • Post links: X post URLs pasted, shared with, or added to the download queue.
  • Public post information: Public usernames, captions, thumbnails, media URLs, available resolutions, file sizes, and media metadata needed for downloads.
  • Download information: Queue entries, download status, selected media options, and locally stored download records.
  • Device-stored content: Images and videos downloaded through the App.
  • Subscription information: Purchase and subscription entitlement status needed to provide Pro features.

The App does not require users to provide their X password for its core download functionality.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Organize and manage post links in the download queue.
  • Retrieve available media information and download options.
  • Download and save supported images and videos.
  • Display download progress, errors, saved files, and history.
  • Detect duplicate post entries where supported.
  • Verify subscription status and enable the appropriate Free or Pro features.
  • Maintain the reliability and security of the App where applicable.

We do not sell users' personal information.

4. Third-Party Services and Media Processing

To retrieve public post metadata and available media, the App may communicate with X-related services or third-party media resolution providers, depending on the implementation and availability of those services.

When a user submits an X post link, the link and associated technical request information may be transmitted to the relevant provider to retrieve media information or download URLs. Those providers may process requests under their own privacy policies and terms.

Google Play Billing may be used to process subscription purchases and verify subscription status. Payment credentials are handled by Google and are not collected directly by the App.

If Firebase, analytics, crash reporting, or other third-party SDKs are enabled in the distributed version, those services may process technical information according to their respective privacy policies.

5. Local Storage and Downloaded Files

The App may store queue entries, download history, preferences, and related application data on the user's device.

Downloaded media files are saved to the configured download location. Their visibility and accessibility depend on Android storage permissions, the selected location, and device settings.

Uninstalling the App may remove some locally stored application data. Downloaded files saved outside the App's private storage may remain on the device until the user deletes them.
